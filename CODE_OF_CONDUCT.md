# Code of Conduct

ActionParity is committed to a respectful, harassment-free community.

Participants must:

- discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people;
- welcome questions and differing levels of experience;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing credentials or personal data;
- give constructive, actionable review;
- avoid discriminatory, threatening, sexualized, or deliberately disruptive conduct.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct threatens the safety or effectiveness of the community.

Reports should be sent privately to the current maintainers through the security contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when public discussion would expose a person or sensitive information.

Enforcement decisions should be proportional, documented privately, and made without commercial favoritism.
